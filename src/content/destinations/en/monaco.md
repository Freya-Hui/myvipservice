Monaco is one of the smallest sovereign states in the world — barely two square kilometers — which means almost everything worth seeing is within a short walk or a five-minute drive of everything else.

## Getting There

There's no airport in Monaco itself; Nice Côte d'Azur (NCE) is the gateway, about 30 minutes by road along the coast. A helicopter shuttle also runs between Nice airport and Monaco's heliport in around seven minutes, which is less a novelty than a genuinely practical option when a client is short on time or arriving with a group.

## The Districts

Monte-Carlo is the part most visitors picture — the casino, the grand hotels, the harbour lined with yachts. La Condamine sits around the port itself, busier and more everyday than Monte-Carlo. Monaco-Ville, on the rock (Le Rocher), is the old town, home to the Prince's Palace and the cathedral, and the one part of Monaco with real medieval-era character. Fontvieille, the newer reclaimed-land district, is quieter and largely residential. Because the whole principality is so compact, moving between them rarely takes more than ten minutes.

## What Monaco Is Actually Known For

The Casino de Monte-Carlo, designed by Charles Garnier — the same architect behind the Paris Opera — is as much an architectural landmark as a gambling destination, and worth a look even if you don't play. The harbour is the other center of gravity, especially during the Monaco Yacht Show and Grand Prix, when it fills with vessels most visitors only see in photographs. The Grand Prix itself, run through the streets in May, is one of the few races anywhere still held on a genuine city circuit rather than a purpose-built track.

## Practical Notes

Grand Prix week sells out hotels and pushes rates up across the entire Riviera, not just Monaco — book well ahead if that's the goal, or plan for the weeks just before or after if you'd rather see Monaco without the crowds and premium pricing. Monaco makes an easy day trip or short stay paired with Nice or the wider Riviera, given how short the transfers are in every direction.

Provence is less a single destination than a collection of small regions — the hilltop villages of the Luberon, the wine country further west, the lavender plateaus, and the coast beyond. Most trips only cover one corner well, which is usually the right call.

## Getting There

Marseille Provence Airport (MRS) has the widest choice of international connections; Avignon's TGV station puts you roughly two and a half to three hours from Paris by high-speed rail, which is often faster door-to-door than flying and connecting. Either way, a car or private driver is the practical way to move between villages once you've arrived — public transport between hilltop towns is sparse.

## The Villages Worth Knowing

Gordes, Roussillon and Ménerbes anchor the Luberon and sit within 20–30 minutes of each other, making them a natural base for a multi-day stay without changing hotels. Gordes is the most photographed, built up a rocky outcrop with views across the valley; Roussillon sits on ochre cliffs that give the whole village a distinct rust-red tone; Ménerbes is quieter, with fewer day-trippers. L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue, known for its Sunday antiques market, sits lower in the valley and makes a good half-day away from the hill towns.

## Wine Country

Provence is best known internationally for rosé, produced across appellations including Côtes de Provence and Bandol, the latter also known for structured reds built on the Mourvèdre grape. Estate visits range from a quick tasting to a full afternoon walking the vines with the winemaker before lunch — the difference is almost entirely about how much time you give it.

## Practical Notes

July and August are lavender season in the fields around Valensole and bring the heaviest heat and crowds of the year; late May through June and September offer similar scenery with noticeably more breathing room. Cash is still useful at village markets even where cards are increasingly accepted, and many restaurants close one or two days a week — worth checking before planning a meal around a specific address.

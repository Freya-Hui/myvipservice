Tell us the day's schedule and we'll run it with the same car and the same driver — the airport run, a dinner, a late return — instead of booking each leg separately and re-explaining it every time.

## Vehicles

For a quiet, private ride or a business meeting, the Mercedes S-Class works well for two. Travelling with family or more luggage, the Mercedes V-Class gives more room, seating up to seven, in either a standard layout or with reclining aviation-style seating. Every configuration is available by the hour, by the day, or for the length of the trip.

## Licensing and Language

Every vehicle and driver is fully licensed under local transport regulations — in France, that means a VTC (Voiture de Transport avec Chauffeur) licence; every other country we serve carries its own equivalent. It's a regulated, accountable service, not an informal arrangement. Drivers are comfortable in Chinese, English and French as standard — say where and when in Chinese and that's enough; no need to translate an address yourself. Other languages can be arranged on request.

## The Cannes Film Festival

During the festival, the Croisette and the surrounding restricted zones only admit vehicles carrying an official pass. Part of the fleet holds the festival's official accreditation, so cars can drive directly to the controlled entrances near the red carpet — no getting out and walking in from the perimeter.

For the airport specifically, two more private options: [VIP Airport Reception](/en/services/vip-airport-reception/) pairs a chauffeur with a dedicated concierge through fast-tracked customs, and [The Private Terminal Experience](/en/services/private-transportation/private-terminal/) skips the public terminal entirely.

Charter isn't a fixed package — we start with passenger count, luggage and route, then choose the right aircraft, rather than picking a jet and fitting the trip around it.

## Choosing the Aircraft

For a short regional route — Paris to Geneva or London, say — a light or midsize jet is usually enough. Intercontinental legs call for a longer-range aircraft like a Gulfstream or Bombardier, with more cabin space and range. The actual aircraft is confirmed once passenger count, luggage and route are settled, never assumed in advance.

## Connecting With Ground Transport

Whether you're flying private or commercial, arrival meet-and-assist, fast-tracked customs and tax-refund support all follow the same standard — see "Airport Arrival & Departure" and "The Private Terminal Experience" for details.

    "The Hôtel-Dieu served as Marseille's primary hospital for more than eight hundred years. Its present horseshoe-shaped building was extended from 1753 under architect Jacques Hardouin-Mansart de Sagonne — grandson of Jules Hardouin-Mansart, Louis XIV's own architect — with monumental staircases by Esprit-Joseph Brun completed in 1785. Renovated between 1860 and 1866 under architect Félix Blanchet, the hospital was formally inaugurated by Napoleon III on 15 November 1866, and its facades, galleries and staircases were listed as a Historic Monument in 1963.",

The InterContinental Marseille – Hotel Dieu opened on 25 April 2013, on Le Panier hill directly above the Old Port.',

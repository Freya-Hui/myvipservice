Our network reaches into some of Europe's most sought-after addresses, unlocking privileges a standard online booking never can.

In Paris, that means long-standing relationships with the city's Palace hotels and design-led addresses — Four Seasons George V, Plaza Athénée, Ritz Paris, Le Meurice, Hôtel de Crillon, Mandarin Oriental, Shangri-La, Cheval Blanc, La Réserve, The Peninsula and Bulgari Hotel Paris among them — alongside private villas with their own gardens for stays that call for more space and privacy. The same network extends to London, Milan, Rome and other major European cities.

Where genuinely available, a booking can include a complimentary upgrade, early check-in, late check-out, welcome amenities or a priority restaurant reservation — never guaranteed in advance, but requested as a matter of course. Whether it's a landmark hotel in the heart of a capital or a villa with its own garden, each suggestion reflects what we'd choose for our own stay — and every booking includes the kind of introduction that makes a return visit feel personal.

Villas are matched the same way — a private estate on the Côte d'Azur, a walled garden property outside Aix-en-Provence, a chalet in the Alps — each one visited or vetted directly rather than pulled from a listings database. For a family or group travelling together, a villa often works better than block-booking hotel rooms: one kitchen, one pool, one address, and staff who answer to a single point of contact rather than several hotel departments. Private chefs, housekeeping and transport can all be arranged around the property once dates are confirmed.

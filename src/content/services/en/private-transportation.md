From gate-side meet-and-assist to a private terminal, every arrival and departure is met by a dedicated team and a premium chauffeured fleet.

Private jet charter is matched to the journey rather than a fixed aircraft type — Gulfstream, Bombardier and similar aircraft cover everything from a short regional hop to an intercontinental flight. At Paris Charles de Gaulle, clients can be met planeside and taken through a private terminal (FBO), away from the airport's public areas, with tax-refund paperwork handled along the way.

On the ground, a fleet of Mercedes S-Class and V-Class vehicles is on call 24/7, with a choice between an experienced international chauffeur or a Chinese-speaking concierge driver. Every transfer is planned around the rest of the day's schedule rather than booked as a one-off.

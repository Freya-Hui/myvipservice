Travelling with family changes the brief, not the standard — every itinerary can be shaped around childcare, pace and age-appropriate activities.

For families staying longer or returning each summer, we arrange access to European boarding-school summer programmes — schools such as Beau Soleil and Le Rosey in Switzerland among them — combining language learning with outdoor activities, alongside academic study tours and campus visits for older children weighing future applications.

Sport and creativity are handled the same way: football academy training, tennis masterclasses and equestrian coaching matched to a child's level, and hands-on workshops — museum sketching sessions, pottery, jewellery-making or patisserie — arranged with artisans and instructors chosen for that trip. Because these programmes and instructors vary by season and by city, the exact mix is confirmed once we know your dates and your children's ages.

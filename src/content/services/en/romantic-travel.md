A romantic trip works best when it is shaped around two people rather than a list of sights — the pace, the settings and the privacy matter more than how much ground gets covered.

Pre-wedding photography is one of the most requested parts of this service: a full day, or two, built around Paris's classic backdrops and quieter alternatives away from the crowds. The photographers and stylists we work with have portfolios that include major fashion campaigns and shoots for well-known public figures — arranged and credited privately, in keeping with how that work is usually handled, rather than published here as a named partnership.

Beyond photography, this covers the full arc of a couple's trip: a private dinner on a rooftop or riverside terrace, a discreetly planned proposal, a honeymoon itinerary that favours quiet addresses over crowded ones. Exact settings and availability are confirmed once we know your dates.

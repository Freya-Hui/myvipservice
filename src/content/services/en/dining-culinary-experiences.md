Where a standard reservation line falls short, our relationships open the door — including tables that are otherwise fully booked.

For a private occasion, a Michelin-starred chef can be invited to cook at your residence, villa or château rather than the other way around — reserved for moments that call for more than a restaurant booking. Rare vintages from leading wine estates can also be sourced for meals with particular significance, drawing on the same network of Bordeaux and Burgundy producers used for our wine-focused itineraries.

Day to day, the same network secures tables at hard-to-book and Michelin-starred addresses across Europe's major cities — the kind of reservation a standard booking line, however persistent, usually can't deliver on short notice.

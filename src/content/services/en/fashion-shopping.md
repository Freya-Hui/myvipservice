Fashion and jewellery access is where our network runs deepest — years spent building relationships across the maisons our clients already know by name, from the high-jewellery houses of Place Vendôme to the ateliers behind haute couture's front row.

That reach spans three areas. In high jewellery: Van Cleef & Arpels, Cartier, De Beers, Graff, Harry Winston, Cindy Chao, Boucheron, Piaget, Buccellati and Damiani. In fine watchmaking: Patek Philippe, F.P. Journe, Richard Mille and Audemars Piguet. In haute couture: Giorgio Armani, Elie Saab, Georges Hobeika, Zuhair Murad and Stephane Rolland. Availability is always confirmed case by case — a private presentation, a fitting, or seating at a show — never assumed in advance.

Alongside those relationships, we arrange personal shopping appointments at the Avenue Montaigne and Rue Saint-Honoré flagships, including Hermès, Chanel, Brunello Cucinelli and Loro Piana, with private lounge access and tax-refund support at the major department stores. Around Cannes, Berlin and Venice, hair, make-up and photography support can be arranged for red-carpet moments.

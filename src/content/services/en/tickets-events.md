From museum reservations to hard-to-find seats at a major fixture, our team looks into what's genuinely available and keeps you informed at every step.

## Sporting Events

The European sporting calendar runs from Six Nations Rugby in February through Wimbledon and the Tour de France in July to the closing rounds of the UEFA Champions League in the spring. We pursue access and hospitality case by case for the fixtures that matter to a given trip:

- **Roland-Garros** — seats and hospitality at the French Open
- **Wimbledon** — where arranged
- **Formula 1** — the Monaco and British Grand Prix
- **Six Nations Rugby**
- **The Tour de France**
- **The London Marathon**
- **The Open Championship** — golf
- **The 24 Hours of Le Mans**

Access and pricing are confirmed on request and cannot be guaranteed in advance.

## Private Tennis Coaching

Where it can be arranged, we set up one-to-one sessions with retired professional players — a private hour on court rather than a group clinic.

## Attractions and Museums

From timed entry at the Louvre to a guided visit at Versailles, we handle the booking so a full day isn't lost standing in line — this also covers other major sights and museums across the cities on your itinerary.

## Theatre and Concerts

We look into tickets for theatre, concerts and cultural events in London, Paris and other major European cities, confirming what's genuinely available rather than promising a specific show in advance.

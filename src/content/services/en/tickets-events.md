From museum reservations to hard-to-find seats at a major fixture, our team looks into what's genuinely available and keeps you informed at every step.

The European sporting calendar runs from Six Nations Rugby in February through Wimbledon and the Tour de France in July to the closing rounds of the UEFA Champions League in the spring — access and hospitality are pursued case by case for the fixtures that matter to a given trip, Roland-Garros and Formula 1's Monaco and British Grand Prix among the ones most often requested. Access and pricing are confirmed on request and cannot be guaranteed in advance.

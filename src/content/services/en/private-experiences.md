From a landmark wedding to a milestone celebration, our production team manages venue, design and every detail of the day.

Venue access spans Paris opera houses and private châteaux in the surrounding region, paired with creative direction from French floral designers and visual stylists who build a bespoke theme around the occasion rather than a template. The same production team coordinates hair and make-up, photography and entertainment, so nothing is arranged separately on the day itself.

Beyond weddings, the same approach applies to milestone birthdays, anniversaries and coming-of-age celebrations, plus corporate retreats and client-appreciation events delivered end to end — from concept through on-site production, with strict attention to guest privacy throughout.

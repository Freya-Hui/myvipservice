Courchevel gets more winter offers every season, but this is the first time one has genuinely linked the Alps to Paris in a single stay. Fouquet's Courchevel (formerly Hôtel Barrière Les Neiges) has built this season's package around a straightforward premise — stay 5 nights or more, and choose one of three perks, one of which is two free nights at Fouquet's Paris. Come down from the mountain and you don't have to unpack twice — the trip simply continues into Paris.

## How the offer works

The package is built for two guests, with a minimum stay of 5 consecutive nights or more at Fouquet's Courchevel, in room categories ranging from Superior Rooms to Deluxe Suites depending on the perk (details below). The three perks are mutually exclusive — you choose one, and it can't be combined with any other offer at the hotel. Worth deciding in advance which extension actually suits the trip — two more nights in Paris, a ski pass, or a better room — before choosing which tier to book.

## Choosing between the three perks

**Perk one: two free nights at Fouquet's Paris.** Includes breakfast, a VIP welcome on arrival, then straight into two nights at Fouquet's Paris (breakfast included), in the equivalent room category to Courchevel. Rates start from roughly €2,400/night, in room categories from Superior Room to Deluxe Suite. Of the three, this is the one for a trip meant to cover both the mountain and Paris in one go — a good fit for a family or couple treating this as the year's main holiday, without having to plan a separate Paris stay afterwards.

**Perk two: a Three Valleys ski pass plus airport transfers.** A Les 3 Vallées ski pass for the full length of the stay, plus private transfers to and from Geneva or Lyon airport, along with access to the spa and the kids' club (Le STUDIO by Petit VIP). Rates start from roughly €2,900/night, in Deluxe Suite and above. This tier suits a trip built around skiing first, where getting transport and lift access sorted in one package matters — the airport-to-hotel leg is often the part of a ski trip most likely to go wrong, and having the hotel handle it directly saves a fair amount of coordination.

**Perk three: a free suite upgrade.** Includes breakfast, a VIP welcome, spa and kids' club access, with a complimentary upgrade of one room category. Rates start from roughly €2,400/night, and this tier has the broadest room eligibility — from Superior Room, Deluxe Room and Privilege Room through to Junior Suite and Deluxe Suite. If the trip is mainly about spending a few extra days on the mountain, with no need for Paris or an additional ski pass, this is the most straightforward value option.

## Which dates the offer covers

All three perks share the same date windows — three are open for the 2026–2027 season:

- 4–19 December 2026
- 9 January – 6 February 2027
- 6 March – 4 April 2027

Christmas–New Year and the February French school holidays, the two traditional peak windows, aren't included, which suggests the offer is mainly designed to draw guests into the quieter shoulder periods either side of peak season. Seen another way, each of these windows has its own advantage: December is early season, quieter and better value; mid-January through early February typically has the most reliable snow; from March, days get longer and temperatures ease, making skiing more comfortable.

## Fouquet's Courchevel itself

The hotel sits right on the Bellecôte piste, in the heart of Courchevel 1850, with direct access to the Three Valleys ski area — table stakes for a grand hotel in this part of the Alps. What actually stays with you is what happens once you're back from the mountain. 22 rooms and 20 suites, open fires, timber panelling, warm fabrics — the solid feel of an Alpine chalet is kept fully intact, and the suites push that feeling even further, closer to a private mountain residence of your own.

The in-house restaurant, Loulou, leans Italian, with terrace tables set out among snow-covered pines — lively without losing a sense of ease.

The spa is built around a 20-metre pool, with treatments from Biologique Recherche, Nooance and Hydrafacial, alongside a scent experience from French fragrance house Kos Paris — the kind of place a post-ski afternoon disappears into.

## If you choose the Paris nights: what Fouquet's Paris is like

In 2026, Fouquet's Paris became the first hotel on the Champs-Élysées to receive the official Palace distinction — France's top hotel classification, above five stars, judged on architectural history, service standard and room quality. Designed by Jacques Garcia, the hotel pairs its Haussmannian exterior with a contemporary French Art Deco interior; around 100 rooms and suites, many with direct views of the Champs-Élysées or the Arc de Triomphe, with gold, velvet and considered decorative detail setting the tone throughout.

![Fouquet's Paris Champs-Élysées Prestige Suite, a gold quilted headboard beside French doors opening onto a wrought-iron balcony](/images/accommodation-fouquets-paris-suite.jpg)

Dining continues the Fouquet's name's own history — the original Fouquet's restaurant, open since 1899, with its red awning and wicker terrace chairs one of the more recognisable sights on this stretch of Paris; the hotel itself also has Le Joy, a restaurant facing a private garden, and a classic cocktail bar with a rooftop terrace that opens in summer — both well known fixtures of Paris nightlife.

![Fouquet's Paris' Le Joy restaurant, a library-style dining area in deep green and walnut tones opening onto a private garden](/images/accommodation-fouquets-paris-le-joy.jpg)
